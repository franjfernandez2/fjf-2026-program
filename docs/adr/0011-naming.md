# ADR 0011: Naming: compound FJF-2026

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decided by:** Francisco Fernández

## Context

The compound and studies appear in every table, report and page.

## Decision

The compound is FJF-2026. The studies are FJF-2026-201 (phase II) and FJF-2026-301 (phase III).

## Alternatives considered

An invented generic name: more narrative, but must not clash with a real medicine. A neutral name such as Compound X: less memorable.

## Consequences

Development-code naming matches industry practice and carries the author's initials discreetly.
