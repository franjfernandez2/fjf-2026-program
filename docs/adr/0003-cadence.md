# ADR 0003: Cadence: near full-time, about 12 weeks

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decided by:** Francisco Fernández

## Context

The schedule depends on available hours. The author is available close to full time.

## Decision

Plan for about 30 hours per week: seven phases (F0 to F6) over about 12 weeks. Work started on 2026-11-11.

## Alternatives considered

10 hours per week (about 34 weeks) and 15 hours per week (about 23 weeks) were considered when availability was unknown.

## Consequences

Each phase ends at a gate with verifiable criteria. If a phase overruns, the next gate moves; tests are never cut to meet a date.
