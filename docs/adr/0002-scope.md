# ADR 0002: Scope: core programme plus extensions E1 and E2

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decided by:** Francisco Fernández

## Context

The dossier defines nine core modules and four optional extensions. Scope must be large enough to show capability and small enough to finish.

## Decision

Build the full core, then extension E1 (replication of the phase III primary analysis in SAS) and E2 (simulation study of MMRM and multiple imputation under missing-not-at-random data). About 340 hours in total.

## Alternatives considered

Core only: about 280 hours, but no SAS evidence. All four extensions (adding E3 group-sequential design and E4 Bayesian Emax): higher risk of not finishing.

## Consequences

E3 and E4 stay out of scope unless reopened by a new ADR after gate 6. A SAS OnDemand for Academics account is needed before E1.
