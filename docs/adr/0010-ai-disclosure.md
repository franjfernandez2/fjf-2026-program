# ADR 0010: AI assistance is declared clearly

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decided by:** Francisco Fernández

## Context

Technical reviewers will ask how the project was built. An undisclosed answer found later would damage the credibility of the whole portfolio.

## Decision

The README and docs/ai-assistance.md state who decides, who programs and who reviews.

## Alternatives considered

A one-line credit without detail. No mention at all.

## Consequences

The same answer must be given in the repository, at interview and on LinkedIn.
