# ADR 0007: Language: English repository, bilingual website

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decided by:** Francisco Fernández

## Context

The industry works in English; the author's website is bilingual.

## Decision

Code, documents, commits and pull requests are in English. Website case pages are in English and Spanish.

## Alternatives considered

Everything bilingual: doubles documentation. Everything in English including the website: loses the Spanish audience.

## Consequences

Explanatory notes for the author may be written in Spanish where that helps understanding; they are not part of the published deliverables.
