# ADR 0036: Plan of extension E2, a simulation study of the efficacy analyses under missing data

- **Status:** Proposed (for the reviewer's review)
- **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **Decided by:** proposed by the programmer; the reviewer chose extension E2 in ADR 0002

## Context

ADR 0002 includes extension E2: a simulation study of the bias and coverage of the MMRM and of
multiple imputation under data that are missing not at random (MNAR). It is the one piece of the
programme with methodological value of its own. The primary analysis of FJF-2026-301 (A1) imputes
the values of patients who leave the study by jump to reference; its supplementary analysis
(E2 of the SAP, the hypothetical estimand) is an MMRM under missing at random. The sample size and
the dry run of the SAP used one dropout mechanism, at random in time and unrelated to the outcome.
Nothing yet says how the analyses behave when dropout depends on the outcome.

## Decision

- **Plan first.** The plan is `docs/sap/e2-simulation-plan.yml`. It is committed, with this ADR,
  before any result of the study is computed; the git history is the evidence, as for the
  integrated safety plan (ADR 0034).
- **Data.** Trials of the planned size of FJF-2026-301 generated by the dry-run generator
  (`R/dry_run_301.R`) from the public **planning values** and public seeds. The study never
  reads the sealed truth, never calls `simulator/` and never reads study data (ADR 0018).
  The only change is how patients leave the study: the generator's random withdrawal is switched
  off and replaced by the mechanisms below.
- **Target.** The treatment-policy difference between FJF-2026 20 mg and placebo in the change of
  HbA1c at week 26, in the full data (nothing missing): the mean of `CHG_FULL` by arm. Its true
  value is the mean over a very large trial of the same generator, with the Monte Carlo error
  reported. The full data are the same in every scenario, so the target is the same.
- **Mechanisms of withdrawal from the study.** A patient leaves with a probability that depends on
  a standardised variable, calibrated so that the share of patients who leave is the stated rate;
  the week of leaving is uniform over the study.
  - MCAR: no dependence.
  - MAR: baseline HbA1c, 1 log-odds per standard deviation. Observed, so ignorable.
  - MNAR (moderate, strong): the unobserved change at week 26, 0.75 and 1.5 log-odds per standard
    deviation; worse HbA1c leaves more.
  - MNAR in the active arm only: 1.5 log-odds per standard deviation, in FJF-2026 20 mg only.
- **Rates.** 6% (the planning value of `study_withdrawal`) and 15% (a stress value outside its
  truth range, to show what a higher dropout does).
- **Analyses.** The same fitting code as the SAP, unchanged: the MMRM on all observed values
  (`fit_mmrm_301()`), multiple imputation with jump to reference (A1, `pooled_ancova_301()`) and
  multiple imputation under missing at random (S1). 150 patients per arm, 150 trials per cell,
  20 imputations, one seed per trial.
- **Measures**, each with its Monte Carlo error: bias against the target, empirical standard
  deviation, mean model standard error, coverage of the 95% interval, and power. The unobserved
  full-data estimate is a reference with no bias by construction.
- **Expectations**, stated before the results and reported as met or not: all three analyses are
  nearly unbiased under MCAR and MAR; the two MAR-based analyses are biased under MNAR, towards
  a larger benefit when worse patients leave more, more so at 15%; jump to reference is
  conservative for the active arm under MNAR in the active arm, and is not expected to recover
  the treatment-policy target under MCAR (it assumes the effect is lost on leaving).
- **Limit.** The conclusions hold for this generator. The mechanisms are chosen to be understood,
  not to resemble a real trial.

## Alternatives considered

- **Real-data patterns of dropout.** None is available and none may be used.
- **More mechanisms and sample sizes.** Costs time without a new lesson; the grid can be extended
  by a later ADR.
- **The sealed simulator.** It is the study's data generator, and the seed is not to be read
  (ADR 0018).

## Consequences

- Results go to `studies/e2/`; the report states which expectations held and which did not.
- Any change to the grid after the first results is labelled post hoc.
